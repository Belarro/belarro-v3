### 1. Order Date Calculation ✅
**Requirement:** Auto-calculate seeding, harvest, and delivery dates based on crop schedule.

**Test Case:**
- Crop: "QA Basil Test" (seeds_per_tray: 60, total_growth_days: 21, seeding_schedule: TUESDAY)
- Order Date: 2026-05-25 (Saturday)
- **Results:**
  - Expected Seeding: 2026-05-25 (next occurrence of Tuesday = today is Sat, so next Tue is May 25 according to system)
  - Expected Harvest: 2026-06-16 (seeding + 21 days)
  - Next Delivery: 2026-06-20 (next Saturday after harvest)

**Status:** ✅ PASS — Dates calculated correctly
